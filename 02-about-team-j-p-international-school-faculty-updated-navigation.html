Chairman's Message

Dear parents

Our vision is simple yet powerful: every child deserves an education that is inclusive, holistic, and future-ready.

We are developing our school around four pillars:

  • Inclusive Education - A place where every learner, regardless of background or ability, feels seen, supported, and celebrated.
  • Holistic Development - Beyond marks, we nurture intellect, health, creativity, and emotional strength.
  • Skill & Vocational Education - From AI to Information technology, we equip students with real-world skills that build confidence and livelihoods.
  • Strong Value System - Knowledge without character is incomplete. Integrity, respect, and service remain at the heart of everything we teach.

As Chairman, my commitment is to create a campus where tradition meets innovation, and where your child grows into a capable, compassionate citizen. Thank you for being part of our journey.

Advocate Amit Chaudhary

Principal's Message

Parul Singh
M Com.,M.A.Edu.,B.Ed.,PGDM

Dear parents and well wishers

J P International school was founded under the aegis of GL Educational Society on 15 April 2010 with the vision to provide an inclusive, holistic and value based education to children. At JPIS every child is special.

We always strive to provide a cohesive environment in the school be it morning assembly, sports activities, yoga classes, karate classes, and a year around different events which are solely managed and organised by the students under the supervision of teachers and we make sure that every child finds its space to express, compete and grow.

  • Academic Excellence: Our students secured outstanding results in the recent Board Examinations, with a 100% pass rate and many scoring above 90%. This success reflects their hard work, your support at home, and our teachers’ dedication in the classroom.
  • Sports, Arts & Expression: From the cricket field to the stage, our children are exploring their talents. With regular coaching in athletics, football, and yoga, plus thriving clubs for music, art, and drama, we ensure every child finds a space to express, compete, and grow.
  • Values That Last: While we celebrate awards and ranks, our greatest achievement is seeing students who are disciplined, empathetic, and responsible. Morning assemblies, shram daan, plantation drive and greater emphasis on value education are woven into daily life so that success is always paired with character.

Thank you for trusting us with your child’s most formative years. Together, we are not just chasing results — we are raising capable, creative, and compassionate individuals.

“Educating minds, nurturing hearts.”

Best regards,

Parul Singh

Principal
