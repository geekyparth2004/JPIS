Voices of the JPIS Legacy

Real journeys, reflections, and gratitude from the students who carry JPIS with them.

CA Finalist

Sneha Garg

Class 2 to Class 12 at JPIS

My name is Sneha Garg, and I am currently a CA Finalist. As I look back on my journey, I feel immense gratitude towards JP International School, where I studied from Class 2 to Class 12 and spent some of the most important years of my life.

Joining the school as a young child, I had little idea of the path ahead. But over the years, JP International School provided me with a strong academic foundation that played a crucial role in shaping my career. The discipline, consistency, and conceptual clarity that I developed during my school years have been extremely helpful in my journey towards becoming a Chartered Accountant.

The teachers at the school were always supportive and encouraging. They not only focused on academic excellence but also instilled values like dedication, hard work, and integrity, qualities that are essential in the field of finance and accounting. Their constant motivation helped me stay focused and determined, even when things felt challenging.

I would especially like to express my heartfelt gratitude to our respected Principal, Mrs. Parul Singh. Her leadership, vision, and dedication have always inspired students like me to aim higher and stay focused on our goals. She created a positive and disciplined environment in the school where every student felt encouraged to grow and excel. Her guidance and encouragement have played a significant role in shaping my mindset and approach towards both academics and life.

Apart from academics, the school environment helped me grow as an individual. Participating in various activities and interacting with peers taught me confidence, communication skills, and teamwork. The friendships and memories I built during these years are truly priceless.

Today, as I work towards achieving my professional goals, I can clearly see how much of my foundation was built at JP International School. I will always be proud to be a part of this institution, and it will forever hold a special place in my heart.
