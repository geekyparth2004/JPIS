Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

When do admissions start for the next academic year?

Admissions typically open in November for the upcoming session starting in April. We recommend applying early as seats are limited.

Is there an admission entrance test?

For Grade 1 upwards, there is a basic entrance assessment to understand the child's academic foundation in English, Mathematics, and Hindi.

Do you provide school transport?

Yes, we have a safe and secure fleet of school buses covering major areas in and around Sikandrabad.

Can we visit the school for a physical tour?

Absolutely! We encourage parents to visit our campus between 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM on working days. Please book a tour through the website or call us.
